## Decisions
- Select both `session_id` and `conversation_id`, not session-only: both were the C3 end-to-end measured arm; session-only has narrower single-call/Pi evidence. The second header is justified by preserving that selected measured wire arm, not by claiming it independently improves hits. (source: request)
- Use the existing cache identity and exact-key fallback, without extra settings or a new fallback mechanism. New conversations remain deliberately cold and reviewers independent. (source: request)
- Use local recording-server deterministic acceptance, with actual packaged CLI Shape/Refine/Build/Review, explicit resume, transient retry and a real Shape working-window reset. No live account or paid comparison during this acceptance. (source: request)
- Preserve caching first and efficient file/context reading second on the settled roadmap; do not dispatch duplicate CACHE-BEST research, disturb independent Studio delivery/frozen Builds, or resume paused Go/014 work. (source: request)
