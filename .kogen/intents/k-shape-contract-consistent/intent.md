# Refuse acceptance that contradicts its contract

Slug: k-shape-contract-consistent
Model: not applicable — one more gate in the existing Shape submission path, a per-invocation record of reviewed candidate hashes and one hash-bound record that approval reads; no new state machine
Acceptance:
- .kogen/intents/k-shape-contract-consistent/acceptance.feature
- features/steps/k_shape_contract_consistent_steps.js
- features/steps/go_driver_support.js

Fix K-SHAPE-CONTRACT-CONSISTENT (Studio kogen issue 42502600-d8e4-4042-97f5-978dde341b1d, "Shaping writes acceptance that contradicts its own Intent"; top Kogen defect, main, 2 Nov 2026). Shape publishes, and approval accepts, acceptance that no correct implementation can pass; the Build implements the change, passes its checks and stops `candidate/build_blocked` at the protected acceptance. Three recurrences within 25 minutes on 2 Nov (kogen v0.1.0-dev.8d10293b): K-STEP-FILE-COLLISION Build f63f53ce (an expected step-file list without the `features/steps/README.md` that `kogen init` writes, and an Intent asserted as "must not be published" although Shape keeps a refused attempt's files), S-ROADMAP-AUTO Build fea7d4b7 (`draft` read on every item of a lane holding a task, while the contract gives `draft` to proposals only; `roadmap.projection` expected at the top level of `studio agent context` while the unchanged Go CLI wraps output as `{format,value,references}`), S-DESIGN-GUIDANCE Build c8537207 (a step calls `studio_artifact_read` without `workspace_id`, which the unchanged MCP schema requires, `addWorkspaceSchema`, cli/cmd/mcp_schema.go:200-211). Readiness cannot see these: the contradicting steps sit behind a step that fails because the feature is missing, so they never run before the Build (about 9% of cells in finding shape-example-check-3ea2e23-screen).

After this change every Shape (native and external `executor: claude-code`) gives each new candidate that readiness accounted for to an independent read-only **contract review**, and refuses the candidate only for findings whose quoted evidence Kogen finds in the cited files and that a second independent **confirmation** confirms. The refusal goes back to the shaper like other invalid submissions; a contradiction kept to the third invalid submission ends Shape `intent/not_ready`, and `kogen intent approve` refuses that unchanged package. This replaces nothing in readiness and is a different design from the reverted example check (d1ffe29, reverted by K-EXAMPLES-REVERT 093ff1f): no model derives expected values, findings must quote both the expectation and the contract it contradicts, unverifiable or unconfirmed findings never refuse, and an incomplete review lets the candidate go on. No new command, flag, option or setting; no limit or timeout is raised.

## When the contract check runs
- In `checkSubmission` (`cli/shape/shape.go`), for a files submission whose written and formatted candidate passed the existing gates (decision check when enabled, step self-check, readiness and the unwritten-tests gate) and whose readiness check ran fresh: after `certify` produced accounted observations or green, before the observations are returned or the candidate is accepted as ready. Questions, blocked reports and submissions refused by an earlier gate get no contract check. Refine is unchanged and runs no contract check.
- Candidate identity is the package hash `kogen intent approve` shows (`project.Hash` over `project.Baseline`) of the written, formatted candidate. Within one Shape invocation a candidate checked without a refusal is not checked again (an acknowledgment of unchanged observations makes no review request). A candidate refused by the contract check and resubmitted unchanged is refused again with the same feedback text right after writing and fixers, before the decision check, step self-check and readiness: no provider request, no readiness run and no new `contract_review` event, only its `submission_refused` event.

## The review and the confirmation calls
- Native: each is one fresh conversation on the shape role and route (same model and effort, its own cache identity, no other conversation state). Its tools are exactly `read_file`, `list_files` and `search_text` (the existing read-only implementations over the checkout, with the existing 16384-byte result display) plus its submit tool: `submit_contract_review` for the review, `submit_contract_confirmation` for the confirmation (strict schemas below). Kogen runs the read-only calls and continues the conversation until the submit tool is called; at most 8 model requests and 32 read-only tool calls per conversation. Progress lines `kogen: checking the contract` and `kogen: confirming contract findings`; their tokens are added to the usage total Shape prints.
- External (`executor: claude-code`): one fresh read-only session per review and per confirmation, with the instructions and input below as its prompt and the same schema as its structured result (executor directories `executor-contract-review-<n>` and `executor-contract-confirm-<n>` beside the decision check's); their rounds count in the usage line's round count. A refusal reaches the external shaper as its next round's feedback.
- Incomplete review: an answer that is not exactly one valid payload of the submit tool, a text-only answer, reaching either bound, an external session ending without a result, or a request above the existing 524288-byte request ceiling ends that review or confirmation as `incomplete`: the candidate goes on unrefused, progress `kogen: contract check incomplete (<why>); the candidate goes on` and the event records `result` `incomplete` with `reason`. Provider failures follow the existing Shape provider-failure path, as the decision check's do. There is no retry.
- Review payload: `{"findings":[{"scenario":string,"expectation":{"path":string,"quote":string},"contract":{"path":string,"quote":string},"why":string}]}`; every field required, no other field. Confirmation payload: `{"verdicts":[{"finding":integer,"verdict":"confirmed"|"refuted","why":string}]}`.
- Review instructions, exactly:
  `You check one candidate Intent before any Build is spent on it. Find acceptance expectations that no correct implementation of the Intent can pass.` / `Read-only: use only read_file, list_files and search_text, then call submit_contract_review once.` / `An expectation is an assertion, an expected value or output, or a call with its arguments, in the candidate acceptance.feature or in a step file the Intent lists under Acceptance. Report a finding only when the expectation contradicts the candidate intent.md (a rule, table, example or boundary it states) or the behaviour of repository code or text that the candidate does not change and the Intent keeps unchanged (for example an output envelope, a required argument, or a file another command writes).` / `The readiness observations show how the unchanged tree behaved and which steps never ran. A scenario that fails only because the requested feature is missing is not a finding. Do not report style, coverage, weak tests, preferences or anything a correct implementation could satisfy.` / `When unsure, report nothing.` / `For each finding give scenario (the scenario name exactly as in acceptance.feature), expectation (its path and the exact text copied from that file), contract (the path of intent.md or of the unchanged repository file, and the exact text copied from it that the expectation contradicts) and why (what a correct implementation does instead). Answer {"findings":[]} when there is none.` — the six parts joined by single newlines (the `/` separate them here only).
- Confirmation instructions, exactly: `You independently verify reported contradictions in one candidate Intent before any Build is spent on it.` / `Read-only: use only read_file, list_files and search_text, then call submit_contract_confirmation once.` / `For each numbered finding, inspect the cited expectation and contract yourself. Answer confirmed only when a correct implementation of the Intent cannot pass the expectation because of the cited contract; otherwise answer refuted. Give one verdict per finding with its number and a short why.` — joined by single newlines.
- Review input, one user message: `Slug: <slug>`, then for each section `\n\n# <title>\n\n<content with trailing newlines removed>`, in this order: `Candidate intent.md`; `Candidate acceptance.feature`; `Candidate step file <path>` for each step file listed under `Acceptance:` that is a file of the candidate package (proposed in this Shape), in Acceptance order; `Readiness observations` with the readiness observations text of this candidate (the text written to its `observations-<n>.txt`), or `All scenarios pass now.` when readiness is green. Files as written and formatted on disk.
- Confirmation input: the review input, then `\n\n# Findings to verify\n\n` and one line per evidenced finding in returned order, joined by single newlines: `<n>. scenario: <scenario> | expectation: <expectation.path>: <expectation.quote> | contract: <contract.path>: <contract.quote> | why: <why>`, n from 1.

## Mechanical rules (Kogen decides)
Normalize a text by turning each run of spaces, tabs, CR and LF into one space and trimming (the existing `normalizeWhitespace`; `containsNormalized` compares both sides normalized). A finding is **evidenced** when all hold, otherwise **unevidenced**:
- `scenario`, normalized, equals the name of a Scenario or Scenario Outline of the candidate `acceptance.feature`;
- `expectation.path` is the candidate `acceptance.feature` or a step file listed under the candidate's `Acceptance:`, and `expectation.quote` is nonempty and contained in that file as written on disk;
- `contract.path` is the candidate `intent.md` with `contract.quote` nonempty and contained in it, or a file tracked at HEAD that is not a file of the candidate package, with `contract.quote` nonempty and contained in its HEAD content;
- `why` is nonempty.
Unevidenced findings are recorded only: never sent to confirmation, never fed back, never refusing. Without evidenced findings there is no confirmation call. A verdict counts for finding n when n is 1..(number sent) and it is the first verdict for n; a finding without a counted verdict is refuted. Only **confirmed** findings refuse.

With one or more confirmed findings nothing is published for this submission and its feedback (the tool result; external: the next round's feedback) is exactly the headline
`Contract check: <N> acceptance expectation(s) contradict the Intent or code it keeps unchanged, so no correct implementation can pass them; nothing was published. Correct each expectation, or the Intent where the Intent is what is wrong, so that a correct implementation passes; do not delete or weaken a scenario to pass this check. Resubmitting this candidate unchanged is refused again.`
(N = confirmed findings) followed by one line per confirmed finding in returned order, joined by single newlines with nothing after the last:
`- <scenario> | expectation: <expectation.path>: <expectation.quote> | contract: <contract.path>: <contract.quote> | why: <why> | confirmed: <the counted verdict's why>`
with every value normalized. It is an invalid submission under SHP-4: one `submission_refused` event with gate `contract_review` whose problems are those item lines, it counts on the shared invalid-submission counter, accumulated proposals stay, the written files stay as after other not-ready feedback, the candidate supplies no readiness evidence and is never acknowledgeable. At the third invalid submission Shape ends as today with `intent/not_ready: the Intent is not ready to build` and the item lines (exit 1).

Each review that ran records one event in the shaping `events.ndjson`: `{"event":"contract_review","package":"<hash>","result":"clean"|"refused"|"incomplete","findings":<n>,"evidenced":<n>,"confirmed":<n>,"items":[{"scenario","expectation","contract","why","evidenced":bool,"verdict":"confirmed"|"refuted"|null,"confirmation":<why>|null}]}` (plus `"reason"` when incomplete). `refused` means at least one confirmed finding; a review whose findings are all unevidenced or refuted is `clean`. These events are what a hidden-pass measurement counts.

## Approval of a package Shape left contradicting
When Shape ends `intent/not_ready` and its last refused submission was refused by gate `contract_review`, it writes `.kogen/local/shaping/<slug>/contract-contradictions.json` (0600, in the existing 0700 shaping directory): `{"sha256":"<package hash of the files left on disk>","lines":[<the item lines>]}` and a newline. Shape of that slug ending ready removes it. `kogen intent approve <slug>` (card and hash forms), right after computing the current package hash and before the changed-since-approval, hash-prefix and readiness checks, refuses when the record parses and its `sha256` equals the current hash: `intent/contract_contradicts: Shape's contract check found acceptance expectations that contradict the Intent or code it keeps unchanged; correct the Intent's files, or shape it again with kogen intent shape <slug> <request-file>` followed by its lines, each printed indented by two spaces as error lines are (exit 1). It writes no approval marker and changes nothing. A record whose hash differs (the files were edited) or that does not parse does not affect approval; legacy `example-contradictions.json` records stay ignored.

## Decisions
- Shaping, or approval, does not accept an Intent whose scenarios contradict its own contract or the behaviour of code the Intent keeps unchanged; the contradiction is caught before any Build attempt is spent (source: request)
- It must not over-refuse: correct Intents keep passing, and the hidden-pass effect is reported because the previous approach was reverted for that cost (source: request)
- Acceptance includes the three real cases (K-STEP-FILE-COLLISION f63f53ce, S-ROADMAP-AUTO fea7d4b7, S-DESIGN-GUIDANCE c8537207) as regression evidence: each contradicting pair is refused or corrected at shape/approve time (source: request)
- No raised limits or timeouts, and no new CLI command or flag (source: request)
