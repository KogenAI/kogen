## Notes on scope boundary
- "Controller integrity (stale or contradictory binding)" (Appendix A: 10 Builds/156 min) and "Exhausted at check" (18 Builds/552 min, mostly the shared offline/paid retry-budget rule, P1) are real, larger costs, but they fire from `lib/kogen/build/verification.ex` cycle settlement and the `verification_retries` budget, i.e. **after** the first Developer turn is already underway/settling — outside this audit's "before or around the first Developer turn" boundary. Flagging for whoever owns that area, since P1 in the mining doc is the single largest ranked fix (200-859 min).
- No scenario-tracking record in `.kogen/runtime/scenario-tracking/*/record.json` carries an admission-time failure reason; every one of the 133 records was created by `Tracking.new`, which only runs *after* Contract/VerificationPlan/Harness-bindings admission succeeds. This means genuine pre-tracking admission refusals (clean worktree, complete-absent, Jev key, budget, lock) are systematically invisible to tracking-based mining — their absence from the incident data is not proof they never fire, only that Kogen keeps no record of them.
