Add/update these guarded tests and their fake paths, or preserve a separately writable mutation fixture.

- **[BLOCKING]** The proposed permissions conflict with the controller’s frozen-entry comparison and cleanup. `approved_unchanged/1` compares complete package entries, including modes, against the original `approved_entries` (`lib/kogen/build.ex:181-226`); `post_developer_inputs_unchanged/1` invokes that check before any verification (`lib/kogen/build.ex:1179-1183`). Also, automatic publication removal calls `git worktree remove` without restoring write permission (`lib/kogen/build/workspace.ex:587-600`), and manual deletion has the same omission (`lib/kogen/build/workspace.ex:830-852`). Normalize the intentional mode difference while retaining tamper detection, and restore permissions before every removal/rollback path.

The current transport-error branch stops immediately on the guard result (`lib/kogen/build.ex:762-772`); a shared tagged-error rework dispatcher could accidentally resume these failures.
